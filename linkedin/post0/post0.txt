Most "AI recruiting" tools rank resumes on keyword overlap, then hand you a confidence score with no reasoning behind it. Hiring managers don't trust those numbers, and they're right not to — the tool was never trained on whether anyone it picked actually worked out.

We do it the other way around.

Today the work is human. Our recruiters source, vet, and match candidates on skills, fit, and intent before a profile ever reaches you. You get vetted, ready-to-talk shortlists in days, not weeks.

That human work also produces something scraped resumes never could: every placement is a labeled outcome. We know who got hired and whether they stuck. It's a slow, expensive signal — which is exactly why a keyword matcher can't fake it.

That data is what trains Vettic AI. Because every score is anchored to a real result, we can show the skill, experience, and intent signals behind it, plus the past matches it learned from. A score you can read and challenge, not take on faith.

Our team is people who've spent years in HR, working alongside engineers who've run large-scale systems. You need both halves to make that loop real.
